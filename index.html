Why Students Choose ExamCompass Africa

ExamCompass Africa (examcompass.africa) is Nigeria's largest peer-to-peer educational repository built for university and college students. Studying for university semester examinations, JAMB UTME, or Post UTME can be stressful without the right study materials.

ExamCompass Africa bridges the gap between study preparation and academic performance by offering direct access to past questions, detailed course summaries, lecture PDF notes, and simulated Computer-Based Test (CBT) mock exams for top Nigerian institutions like OAU, UNILAG, UI, UNIBEN, FUTA, and UNN.

✓

Free Past Questions: Verified semester exam questions with step-by-step solutions.

✓

Earn Rewards & Tokens: Upload handwritten class notes and earn cash directly.

✓

Interactive CBT Mocks: Practice timed JAMB, Post UTME, and faculty tests online.

✓

Multi-Campus Coverage: Specialized content for all major federal and state universities.
