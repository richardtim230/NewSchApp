Why Students Choose ExamGuard

ExamGuard (registered as ExamGuard, also referenced as ExamCompass — examguard.com.ng) is Nigeria's largest peer-to-peer educational repository built for university and college students. Studying for university semester examinations, JAMB UTME, or Post UTME can be stressful without the right study materials.

ExamGuard bridges the gap between study preparation and academic performance by offering direct access to past questions, detailed course summaries, lecture PDF notes, and simulated Computer-Based Test (CBT) mock exams for top Nigerian institutions like OAU, UNILAG, UI, UNIBEN, FUTA, and UNN.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Are the past questions free?

Many university past questions, lecture notes, and general study materials on ExamGuard are completely free to download. Premium or specialized solutions can be unlocked using ExamGuard tokens.

Q. How do I earn tokens on ExamGuard?

You earn tokens whenever you upload quality handwritten class notes, lecture summaries, or solved past questions that other students unlock. You can also participate in daily CBT micro-tasks.

Q. How do I withdraw my tokens to bank cash?

Simply navigate to your ExamGuard wallet, click "Withdraw", enter your Nigerian local bank account details, and your accumulated tokens convert directly into real cash.

Q. Is ExamGuard free to join?

Yes, student registration is 100% free! Once registered, you are awarded welcome tokens to explore initial academic resources immediately.
