How It Works

A simple, transparent guide to earning rewards through our verification process.

Welcome to OAU ExamGuard's verification system. We've designed this process to be straightforward, rewarding, and enjoyable. In just a few steps, you can complete verification, connect with amazing partner offers, and start earning rewards today.

1

Click on Any Offer

Start by browsing through our featured offers displayed on the verification page. You'll see a variety of premium deals and exclusive offers tailored for engaged users like you.

Simply click on any offer card or the "Explore Now" button to get started. It doesn't matter which one you choose — each click represents genuine engagement with our trusted partner brands.

💡 Tip: You can click either the offer card itself or the button. Both will trigger the verification timer.

2

Timer Activates Automatically

Once you click, our verification timer springs into action, counting down from 30 seconds. The countdown is displayed prominently at the top of your screen with a visual progress bar to track your progress.

During this phase, keep the page open and stay engaged with the offer content. This is an important part of the verification process and helps us ensure authentic user interaction with partner content.

⏱️ Remember: Keep the page visible and active. The timer will pause if you navigate away or switch to another tab.

3

Halfway Pause — Second Click

When you reach the halfway point (15 seconds), the timer automatically pauses. You'll see a friendly notification asking you to click on another offer to continue. This step ensures you're genuinely engaged with our partner content.

Don't worry — it's as simple as before. Click any offer (you can click the same one again or try a different one). The system is flexible and designed around your preferences.

✨ Flexibility: You can click the same offer multiple times or explore different ones. Choose what interests you!

4

Complete Verification

After your second click, the timer resumes and continues the countdown. Once it reaches zero, your verification is officially complete! The status updates on your screen, and you'll unlock access to your reward.

You'll see a celebratory message confirming your successful verification along with a button to claim your rewards.

🎉 Success: Your verification is complete and your reward is ready to claim!

5

Claim Your Reward

With verification complete, it's time to claim your reward. Click the "Get Activation Link & Claim Points" button to proceed to your unique reward activation page.

Your points accumulate across all completed verification tasks. The more you engage, the more you earn. These points can be converted to cash rewards or used toward exclusive benefits in the OAU ExamGuard ecosystem.

💝 Rewards: Points accumulate from every completed task. Track your total in the task center!

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use mobile or should I use desktop?

Our verification system works seamlessly on both mobile and desktop devices. All features — timer, offers, and rewards — are fully responsive and optimized for your device's screen size.

What happens if I leave the page during verification?

The timer pauses if you navigate away or switch tabs. Simply return to this page, and you can continue from where you left off. You won't lose progress on partially completed verifications.

Do I need to purchase anything from the offers?

No, you don't need to make any purchases. Verification is about viewing and engaging with partner content. Simply explore the offers for the required duration to complete the process.

How are my rewards calculated?

Each completed verification task awards points based on engagement quality and partner value. Your total points accumulate in your account and can be converted to cash or exclusive benefits. Higher engagement consistently earns you more rewards.

Is my data secure and private?

Absolutely. We use industry-standard encryption and follow strict privacy protocols. Your interaction data is used exclusively for verification purposes and partner analytics. We never sell or share your personal information with third parties without consent.
