How It Works 🎯

A simple, transparent guide to earning your rewards

1

Click on Any Offer

Browse through our featured offers displayed on the main verification page. Click on any offer card or button that catches your interest. Each click represents your engagement with our partner brands.

💡 Tip: You can click on the offer card itself or the "Explore Now" button — both will trigger the verification process.

2

Timer Activates Automatically

Once you click an offer, our verification timer starts counting down from 30 seconds. The timer displays prominently at the top with a visual progress indicator. Your job is to stay engaged with the offer content during this time.

⏱️ Important: Keep the page open and visible. The timer pauses if you navigate away or switch tabs.

3

Halfway Pause (Second Click)

When the timer reaches the halfway point (15 seconds), it automatically pauses. You'll receive a prompt to click on another offer to demonstrate continued engagement. This step ensures genuine interaction with our partner content.

✨ Pro Tip: You can click any offer during this pause — same offers or different ones. The system is flexible!

4

Complete Verification

After your second click, the timer resumes and counts down to zero. Once it reaches zero, your verification is complete! The status updates to show completion, and you'll unlock access to your reward activation link.

🎉 Reward: A unique activation link will appear that you can use to claim your points and unlock additional benefits.

5

Claim Your Reward

Once verification is complete, click the "Get Activation Link & Claim Points" button. This opens your unique reward link where you can finalize your earnings and unlock exclusive benefits. Your participation helps connect quality partners with engaged users like you.

💝 Benefit: Each completed verification earns you points that accumulate across multiple tasks. Track your progress in the task center!

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use mobile or must I use desktop?
Our verification works seamlessly on both mobile and desktop devices. The timer, offers, and reward system are fully responsive and optimized for your device's screen size.
What happens if I leave the page during verification?
The timer pauses if you navigate away or switch tabs. Simply return to this page and continue from where you left off. You won't lose progress on partially completed verifications.
Do I need to visit the partner sites?
You may be redirected to partner offers when you click, and this is part of the verification process. Review the content for 10+ seconds to help demonstrate genuine engagement. You'll return to claim your reward afterward.
How are my rewards calculated?
Each completed verification task awards points based on engagement quality and partner value. Points accumulate in your account and can be converted to cash or used toward exclusive benefits. Higher engagement = more points!
Is my data secure and private?
Yes! We employ industry-standard encryption and privacy protocols. Your interaction data is used solely for verification purposes and partner analytics. We never sell your personal information to third parties.
