RCCG Sunday School Guide

Weekly Adult Study Manual

LESSON TOPIC

Walking in Divine Covenant Promises

Memory Verse: Genesis 17:7

BIBLE PASSAGE: Genesis 17:1-8
"And when Abram was ninety years old and nine, the Lord appeared to Abram, and said unto him, I am the Almighty God; walk before me, and be thou perfect..."
INTRODUCTION & OUTLINE

A divine covenant is an unchangeable commitment made by Almighty God to His children. In this lesson, we explore how holiness, total obedience, and unwavering faith trigger covenant blessings in everyday life.

LESSON SUMMARY & CONCLUSION

God never breaks His covenant. As believers, walking uprightly before Him positions us for generational elevation and victory.
