Today's Study Manual

LESSON 28

Walking in Divine Light

Memory Verse: "Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path." – Psalm 119:105

Bible Passage: 1 John 1:5-10

Introduction

Light is essential for vision, safety, and direction. In spiritual terms, walking in divine light means living according to God's truth, holiness, and righteousness. God Himself is Light, and in Him is no darkness at all.

Lesson Outlines

1. Characteristics of Divine Light: Purity, absolute truth, revealing hidden things, bringing guidance and hope.
2. Blessings of Walking in Light: Unbroken fellowship with God, spiritual clarity, freedom from stumbling, and cleansing through the Blood of Jesus.
Interactive Question: How can believers maintain unbroken fellowship with God in a world filled with spiritual darkness?
