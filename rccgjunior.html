Junior Church Department Divisions

Classes designed specifically for the emotional, cognitive, and spiritual development of every child.

Seeds of Glory

Ages 2 – 5

Toddlers and preschoolers learning simple truths: "God made me, God loves me, and Jesus is my friend." Filled with songs, picture bibles, and creative play.

Room: Hall A (Ground Floor) Class Lead: Sis. Abigail

Champions of Faith

Ages 6 – 9

Primary school children memorizing scriptures, learning foundational Bible characters, understanding prayer, and practicing obedience and holiness.

Room: Hall B (First Floor) Class Lead: Bro. Emmanuel

Kingdom Ambassadors

Ages 10 – 12

Pre-teens navigating peer pressure, building strong personal devotion, leadership skills, Bible sword drills, and preparing for transition to Teen Church.

Room: Hall C (Second Floor) Class Lead: Deaconess Florence
