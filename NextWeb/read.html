CHAPTER 3

Functions

Small functions are easier to understand, test, and reuse. The first rule of functions is that they should be small. The second rule of functions is that they should be smaller than that.

Functions should do one thing. They should do it well. They should do it only. When a function does more than one thing, it becomes harder to understand, test, and reuse.

A good function name tells you what the function does. A great function name tells you why the function does it.

Keep your functions short, your names descriptive, and your purpose clear.
