A note about access and downloads

UniLibrary prioritizes openly accessible books and papers. Some records are discovery-only, and some books may require borrowing or have publisher restrictions. Downloads are offered only where the provider exposes an accessible file; UniLibrary does not bypass paywalls, lending restrictions or copyright controls.
