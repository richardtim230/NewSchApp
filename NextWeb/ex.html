01 / Problem Statement

ExamGuard Pitch Deck

The Dual Crisis Facing African Campus Life

Academic pressure and financial stress operate in silos, forcing students into trade-offs.

⏱️

Academic Anxiety

The Academic Burden: High-stakes computerized exams (CBT) with zero centralized, reliable prep materials or shared study aids.

👛

Financial Survival

The Economic Strain: Students constantly scramble for income to afford basic accommodation, textbook costs, and meals.

The Broken Market: Student trade and note-sharing are forced onto chaotic, unverified social media group chats, exposing them to transaction fraud.

Result: Students are constantly forced to choose between studying to pass and working to survive.
