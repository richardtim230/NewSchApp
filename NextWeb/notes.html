3. Newton's Laws of Motion

3.1 First Law (Law of Inertia)

An object at rest stays at rest, and an object in motion stays in motion with the same speed and in the same direction unless acted upon by a net external force.

Mathematically:

$$ \sum \vec{F} = 0 \quad \Rightarrow \quad \vec{v} = \text{constant} $$

3.2 Second Law (Law of Acceleration)

The net force acting on an object equals the product of its mass and acceleration.

$$ \sum \vec{F} = m\vec{a} $$

Where:

  • \( \sum \vec{F} \) = Net force (N)
  • \( m \) = Mass (kg)
  • \( \vec{a} \) = Acceleration (m/s\(^2\))
